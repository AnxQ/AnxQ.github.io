Welcome to my academic homepage

Understanding people
through visual perception.

Hello! I am Xiaoqi An at Nanjing University of Science and Technology. My research focuses on computer vision and human pose estimation.

My work explores how to estimate human pose and motion from images and LiDAR point clouds, with an emphasis on efficient representations and robust perception under sparse or imperfect observations.

Robust 3D pose, motion, and mesh reconstruction from point clouds with varying density, noise, and occlusions.
